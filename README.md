# TURATH Heritage Platform

Build a premium, futuristic, scalable web application called:

TURATH

Digital Infrastructure for Tunisian Living Heritage

Tagline:

Notre patrimoine. Notre identité. Notre avenir.

Do NOT build a generic e-commerce marketplace.

The core concept is to create a new digital infrastructure that connects:

Living Heritage + Artisans + Cultural Knowledge + Product Provenance + Verification + Commerce + AI + Heritage Intelligence

The first pilot is TURATH DOUZ, focused on traditional crafts and products from Douz and southern Tunisia.

The architecture must be designed from day one to scale into TURATH TUNISIA and eventually support other countries/regions with living cultural heritage.

1. THE BIG IDEA

TURATH should become the digital layer between:

traditional crafts

artisans

communities

cultural knowledge

products

customers

exporters

researchers

tourism

museums

future institutional partners

The platform must combine five major systems:

HERITAGE KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

PRODUCT PROVENANCE & DIGITAL PASSPORT

ARTISAN & SKILL NETWORK

MARKETPLACE & EXPORT ENGINE

HERITAGE INTELLIGENCE / AI

The platform is NOT a government certification system.

TURATH verification must always be described as:

"Verified according to TURATH's verification criteria."

Never claim government certification, legal ownership of heritage, or official protection unless an actual authorized institution is integrated later.

2. CORE PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The platform should answer five questions:

WHERE DID THIS COME FROM?

Origin and region.

WHO MADE IT?

Artisan identity.

HOW WAS IT MADE?

Materials and technique.

WHAT IS ITS HERITAGE?

Cultural context, history and knowledge.

CAN I TRUST IT?

Evidence, verification and provenance.

Then:

CAN I BUY IT?

Marketplace and international commerce.

13. HERITAGE RADAR

Create an experimental AI feature:

HERITAGE RADAR

Purpose:

Detect potential visual/product similarities between registered heritage patterns/products and external content where external search/API integrations are available.

Workflow:

User/admin submits an image.

System analyzes visual features.

System compares against TURATH Heritage DNA records.

Output:

Potential similarity detected

Similarity score:

87%

Related Heritage DNA:

TN-DZ-PATTERN-0007

Status:

Requires human review

Important:

Never label something as stolen or counterfeit automatically.

Use:

Potential similarity

Human review required

This is an intelligence tool, not a legal judgment system.

For the MVP, implement this with mock/demo analysis if no real AI/image-search API is connected.
